A Beginners Guide to Badminton




A fast-paced racket sport played on a court with a net, played in either singles or doubles, using a shuttlecock.

Rules
  • Only one hit per side is allowed
  • The shuttlecock should land within certain boundaries
  • Players shouldn't touch the net
  • The game is won by the first player to reach 21 points

  • Shuttlecock
